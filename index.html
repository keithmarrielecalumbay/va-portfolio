Process

How I work a case

The four-step authorization workflow — intake to approval, every time.

  1. 01

    Intake

    I take in the request, confirm patient and provider details, and check that the plan, diagnosis and codes line up before anything moves.

  2. 02

    Verify

    I verify eligibility and benefits, then confirm exactly what clinical documentation the payer requires for this specific request.

  3. 03

    Submit

    I prepare and submit the authorization through the payer's platform and log it, so the case is traceable from that moment on.

  4. 04

    Follow through

    I track it to a decision, make the outbound calls that unstick it, and tell you where it stands before you have to ask.
